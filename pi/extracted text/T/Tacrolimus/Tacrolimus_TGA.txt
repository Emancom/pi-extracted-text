Absorption  Data from healthy human subjects indicate that there is little or no systemic exposure to tacrolimus following single or repeated topical application of tacrolimus ointment.  Target trough concentrations for systemic immunosuppression for oral tacrolimus are 5-20 ng/mL in transplant patients. Most adults with atopic dermatitis treated with single or repeated application of tacrolimus   ointment   (0.1%)   had   blood   concentrations   <   1.0   ng/ml.   When   observed,   blood concentrations exceeding 1.0 ng/ml were transient. Systemic exposure increases with increasing treatment areas. However, both the extent and the rate of topical absorption of tacrolimus decrease as the skin heals. In adults with an average of 50% body surface area treated, systemic exposure (i.e. AUC) of tacrolimus from tacrolimus ointment is approximately 30-fold less than that seen with oral immunosuppressive doses in kidney and liver transplant patients. The lowest tacrolimus blood concentration at which systemic effects can be observed is not known.  There was no evidence of systemic accumulation of tacrolimus in adults treated for prolonged periods (up to one year) with tacrolimus ointment.
