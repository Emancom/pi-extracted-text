How effective is this product at preventing pregnancy?  • •  What if I decide I want to get pregnant?  •  Is it okay to use this product if I'm breastfeeding?  •  What types of side effects may I expect while using this product?  • • • As with any birth control method, this product does not prevent pregnancy all the time. This product will work best if you take it exactly as directed. In 8 US clinical trials, approximately 98 out of 100 sexually active women who used this product for a year did not become pregnant in that time. If you decide you want to become pregnant, simply stop taking this product. This product will not delay your ability to get pregnant. Yes. This product is safe and effective in breastfeeding women. Small amounts of progestin may pass into the breast milk; however, no adverse effects have been found on either breastfeeding performance or infant health. When used as directed, this product is safe and effective. The most common side effect is changes in menstrual periods (bleeding). See section 6. Less common side effects may include headaches, dizziness, nausea, increased appetite, abdominal pain, cramps or bloating.
