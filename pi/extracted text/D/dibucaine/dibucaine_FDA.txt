CVS TOPICAL ANALGESIC HEMORRHOIDAL- dibucaine ointment CVS PHARMACY,INC  ----------  CVS Topical Analgesic Hemorrhoidal Ointment  Drug Facts Active ingredient  Dibucaine 1%  Purpose  Local Anesthetic  Use  temporarily relieves pain and itching due to hemorrhoids or other anorectal disorders  Warnings  For external use only. Allergy alert •   certain persons can develop allergic reactions to ingredients in this product. If the symptoms being treated do not subside or if redness, irritation, swelling, pain or other symptoms develop or increase, discontinue use and ask a doctor.  Do not use  in or near the eyes in children under 2 years of age  When using this product  do not use more than directed unless directed by a doctor do not put this product into the rectum by using fingers or any mechanical device or applicator  Stop use and ask a doctor if  bleeding occurs condition worsens or does not improve within 7 days  If pregnant or breast-feeding,  ask a health care professional before use.  Keep out of reach of children.  If swallowed, get medical help or contact a poison control center right away.
