Warnings Choking:  Taking this product without adequate fluid may cause it to swell and block your throat or esophagus andmay cause choking. Do not take this product if you have difficulty in swallowing. If you experience chest pain, vomiting, or difficulty in swallowing or breathing after taking this product, seek immediate medical attention.  ALLERGY ALERT:  This product may cause allergic reactions in people sensitive to inhaled or ingested psyllium.  Ask a doctor before use if you have  abdominal pain, nausea or vomiting a sudden change in bowel habits persisting for 2 weeks  Ask a doctor or pharmacist before use if you  are taking any other drug.
